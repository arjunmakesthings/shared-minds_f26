if two people seem different, how different are they really?

this webpage allows you to view popular us-politicians as vectors in multidimensional space. each 'dimension' is a topic that they've both spoken about, and therefore can be assessed for the polarity of stance that they take. dimensions are selectable & represented live; stances are extracted from linked sources (wikiquote, ontheissues.org, the american presidency project); polarity or similarity is assessed by averaging analyses by two different popular large-language-models.

these placements were made by ai models (claude sonnet and gpt-5) reading the quotes linked in the sidebar. a person did not check them, and a model's reading of what someone means can be wrong. open the sources and judge for yourself.

every quote comes from a linked source (wikiquote, ontheissues.org, the american presidency project) and was checked by a script to appear word for word in that source's text. two ai models then placed each person on each topic, so the stances are machine readings, not facts. "x% similar" depends on which topics you look at, which is the point.
